## Why the theme is mechanical

The player's task is containing danger from powerful automation deployed without enforceable oversight. Permission gates determine whether harmful orders can execute. Manual fallbacks determine whether removing access is survivable. Physical dependencies determine whether one failure spreads. Repairing visible damage does not remove the authority that caused it.

The game illustrates objective misspecification and harmful side effects, topics discussed in [Concrete Problems in AI Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565). It also draws on [Google DeepMind's discussion of specification gaming](https://deepmind.google/blog/specification-gaming-the-flip-side-of-ai-ingenuity/): an objective can be satisfied while the intended outcome is missed.

The city, directives, scores, dependencies, numbers, and proposed interventions are original game abstractions. Neither source establishes the likelihood of this fictional city crisis. A single oversight action is a gameplay compression of independent controllers, accountable approval, and enforceable service limits; it is not a claim that one rule makes real AI safe.

## Intended submission materials

- Checked playable package and exact instructions.
- Complete source and build/version identifier.
- A 3–5-minute demonstration of one consequential choice and its visible chain of effects.
- Short pitch, limitations, asset credits, and AI-tool disclosure.
- Actual verification record, separating numerical, browser, and human observations.
