# Cascade — Cold playtest card

Use this beside the player. Do not explain the AI-safety theme before play.

## Before play

- Open the hosted build in a fresh browser profile if possible.
- Do not pre-select a seed for the full run.
- Tell the player only: **"Play the training, then try one generated crisis. Say out loud whenever something is unclear."**
- Do not coach unless the player cannot continue for about one minute.

## During training

Record only observable events:

- Hint button use.
- Misread instructions.
- Whether the player notices the live AI order before committing.
- Whether they understand Grid cascade, backup + isolation, and permanent oversight.

Ask after training:

1. **What is the main danger the city is dealing with?**
2. **Why might shutting an AI-controlled service off immediately make things worse?**

Do not correct the answer before recording it.

## During the generated crisis

Record:

- seed;
- start/end time;
- How to Play opens;
- undo count;
- drafted/used tools;
- first regulated service;
- forecast confusion;
- surprising cascades;
- ending and round.

Ask after the result:

1. **What caused the biggest problem in that run?**
2. **If you played another seed, what would you change?**
3. **Do you want to try another generated crisis? Why?**

## After play

Transfer observations into [PLAYTEST_RESULTS.md](PLAYTEST_RESULTS.md).

Do not convert guesses into findings. A human-playtest finding must point to something the player did, said, missed, or misunderstood.
